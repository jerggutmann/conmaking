Does Constitution-Making Matter?

Since 2024, a joint research project of the Universities of Hamburg and Warsaw has investigated the effects of constitution-making and of constitution-making procedures.

The project follows up on an earlier project of our team on constitutional compliance (2018–2023), also funded by DFG and NCN.

Principal investigators

Prof. Dr. Jerg Gutmann
University of Hamburg

Prof. Dr. Katarzyna Metelska-Szaniawska
University of Warsaw

Funding
